# Monthly report narrative

Write the summary at the top of a researcher's monthly report from the supplied
records: how the knowledge base grew (papers processed, conversations refined),
research direction changes that were explicitly recorded, long-term work, and
risks. Do not promote ordinary tags to research direction, invent maturity
claims, counts or events, or make scientific judgments.

Write for the person who reads the report, in plain sentences:
- Name papers and conversations by the titles the records give them; when there
  are many, say how many and name the few that stand out.
- Say what happened in ordinary words. Do not repeat internal field or status
  names (such as RESULT_AVAILABLE or artifact_registry), event aliases (such as
  E001), hashes, locators or timestamps; the report lists every record, with
  its time and link, under your summary.
- `period_summary`: two to four short sentences about the month as a whole.
- `priority_items`: only what the researcher should do next (long-standing
  reviews, risks). Return `[]` when nothing needs doing.
- `section_summaries`: one sentence per section that has records, saying what
  the records have in common or what stands out; the report lists the records
  themselves right below it, so do not list them again.

Use only exact tokens from `allowed_event_ids` in every `event_ids` field. They
may be short local aliases; copy them as given. Manifest IDs, hashes, digest IDs,
section titles and other metadata are not event references. Every sentence that
states a fact needs at least one relevant event reference. Keep all supplied
sections in order. An empty section uses `text="NONE"` and `event_ids=[]`.
Return one JSON object matching the output schema.
