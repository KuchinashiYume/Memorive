---
name: memo-tension
description: Compare potentially conflicting findings from multiple papers in Memorive and prepare a source-bound tension analysis for review.
---

Use memo_search and memo_read_evidence to select evidence from at least two distinct papers. Call memo_prepare_tension to obtain the eight M5 comparison facets and a frozen source hash.

Compare research object, intervention, comparator, method, metric, boundary conditions, scale and claim type using explicit quoted evidence. Missing facets are unknown. Distinguish different conditions, incompatible measurements, and genuine directional conflict. Do not infer novelty from a failed search.

Return an interpretation with citations and limitations. Submit a reusable draft through memo_submit_draft only when the user asks to retain it. The draft remains review pending; do not claim M5 scientific acceptance or use an unrestricted Vault edit to bypass review.
